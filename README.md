# 🚀 Arbalest Rocketry
We are a student led and run design team operating under the Lassonde school of engineering since 2020.

# What We do
Each year we compete at **[Launch Canada](https://launchcanada.org/)**, Canada's national student rocketry competition. Teams design, build, and fly rockets to a target altitude, and are judged on engineering, safety, and flight performance.

We focus on developing our members, Through hands-on design, manufacturing, testing, and project management. Preparing students for industry and real-world engineering.

To learn more about our sub-teams, past rockets, and how to join, please visit **[website](https://www.arbalestrocketry.ca/)**.
